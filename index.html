The glacier cross-section

what a million tokens costs, month by month

The shaded blue wedge is your glacier: the gap between what tokens cost to buy and what they cost on your own machines. Prices fall every month (the sun), electricity pushes your floor up (warm ground). The wedge you can harvest has to pay for the toll gate — dev cost plus hardware.

Race to break even

cumulative discounted cost of each path

Building starts in a hole (you paid for chips and engineers on day one) and climbs out only if buying tokens keeps costing more each month than running your own. Where the lines cross is break-even.

Token yield force vectors

value per dollar of token spend, and what pushes it

Token yield is how much value you get per dollar spent on tokens. Buying: yield climbs as prices melt. Building: efficiency gains push yield up while rising electricity drags it down — the downward force vector. Whichever line is higher is the better deal that month.
